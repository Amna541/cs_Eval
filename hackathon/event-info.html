Event Information

The Hackathon will take place from March 15, 2025, to March 17, 2025. Teams will have 48 hours to build innovative tech solutions. Mentorship will be provided throughout the event.

Participants will compete for a chance to win cash prizes, internship opportunities, and a chance to showcase their projects to top tech companies.
